2. Purpose of this application

Code à Cuisine generates recipe suggestions based on ingredients and preferences entered by the user. It also allows users to view and like generated recipes.

3. Data processed during recipe generation

When a recipe is generated, the following information may be processed:

  • ingredients and quantities entered by the user,
  • number of portions and cooks,
  • selected cuisine and dietary preference,
  • selected cooking-time preference,
  • IP address or another technical client identifier,
  • date and number of recipe-generation requests.

This information is processed to generate the requested recipes, operate the application and prevent excessive or abusive use.

Recipe generation is limited per client and per day. For this purpose, the application stores the date, the technical client identifier and the number of requests in the n8n workflow system.

The legal basis for generating requested recipes is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. The prevention of misuse and enforcement of request limits are based on the legitimate interest in operating a secure and reliable application pursuant to Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

4. Artificial intelligence and Google Gemini

The ingredients and preferences entered by the user are transmitted through an n8n workflow to the Google Gemini API. Google processes this information to generate the requested recipe suggestions.

Do not enter names, addresses, health information or other personal or confidential information into the ingredient form.

Further information about Google's processing of personal data is available in the Google Privacy Policy .

5. Firebase and Cloud Firestore

This application uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, services provided by Google. Anonymous authentication is used to create a technical user identifier without requiring the user to provide a name, password or email address.

The following information is stored in Cloud Firestore:

  • generated recipes and recipe instructions,
  • ingredients and selected preferences,
  • estimated nutritional information,
  • anonymous user identifiers,
  • recipe likes,
  • creation timestamps.

Firebase Authentication may additionally process technical data such as IP addresses and browser or device information for authentication, security and abuse prevention.

The processing is necessary to provide the application and its like functionality pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Security and abuse prevention are based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

More information is available in the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation .

6. Data recipients and international processing

Data may be processed by Google and its service providers. Depending on the service, processing may also take place outside the European Union or the European Economic Area. Google states that appropriate safeguards are used where required.

7. Storage period

Generated recipes and their associated data are stored until they are deleted by the project operator or until the project is discontinued.

Request-limit information is stored only for as long as it is required to enforce the daily generation limits. Technical data processed by external service providers may be retained according to their respective privacy and retention policies.

8. Cookies and local storage

The application does not use advertising or marketing cookies. Firebase Authentication may store technically necessary information in the browser to maintain the anonymous user session and provide the like functionality.
